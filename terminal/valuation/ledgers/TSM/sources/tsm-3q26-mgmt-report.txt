Revenue Analysis:
In the third quarter, revenue increased 17.6% quarterover-quarter, as our business was supported by strong
demand for our leading-edge process technologies.
By technology, 2nm process technology contributed 8%
of total wafer revenue in 3Q26, while 3nm, 5nm, and
7nm accounted for 33%, 28%, and 10% respectively.
Advanced technologies (7nm and below) accounted for
79% of total wafer revenue.
By platform, HPC and Smartphone represented 68%
and 21% of net revenue respectively, while IoT,
Automotive, DCE, and Others each represented 4%,
4%, 1%, and 2%.
Sequentially, revenue from HPC, Smartphone, IoT, and
Automotive increased 22%, 10%, 5%, and 23%
respectively. DCE and Others decreased 12% and 10%
respectively from 2Q26.
From a geographic perspective, revenue from
customers based in North America accounted for 79%
of total net revenue in 3Q26, while revenue from Asia
Pacific, China, Japan, and EMEA (Europe, Middle East,
and Africa) accounted for 10%, 5%, 3%, and 3% of
total net revenue respectively.

IV-2 Free Cash Flow

Net cash used in investing activities was NT$266.79
billion in 3Q26, primarily attributable to capital
expenditures of NT$443.21 billion, partially offset by
other investing sources of NT$198.74 billion. The other
investing sources were mainly attributable to U.S.
Treasury Department Investment Tax Credits.
Net cash used in financing activities was NT$160.31
billion, mainly reflecting the payment of 4Q25 cash
dividends of NT$155.59 billion.
As a result, cash position increased by NT$487.67 billion
to NT$3,621.89 billion at the end of 3Q26.
