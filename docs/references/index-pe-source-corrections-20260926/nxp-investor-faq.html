General

What is the difference between NXP Semiconductors N.V. and NXP B.V.?

NXP B.V. is a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of NXP Semiconductors N.V. NXP B.V. is the issuer of our notes and borrower under the Secured Revolving Credit Facility/Forward Start Revolving Credit Facility and acts as the holding company of the operational companies within the NXP group.

When was NXP incorporated?

NXP Semiconductors N.V. was incorporated in the Netherlands in 2006 as a Dutch public company with limited liability (naamloze vennootschap).

Where is NXP's corporate headquarters?

NXP is headquartered in Eindhoven, Netherlands.

When did NXP become a publicly traded company?

NXP stock was first traded on August 6th, 2010. The initial price to the public per ordinary share was $14.00 USD.

How do I get a copy of the latest Annual Report?

I am an NXPI shareholder, and when NXP pays its quarterly dividend, 15% of the dividend is retained by the Dutch Government as a tax withholding. I am a U.S. citizen and don't file a Dutch tax return, how can I obtain a tax refund on the amount withheld by the Dutch Government?

NXP is unable to provide specific tax advice, and we recommend investors consult a financial tax advisor. However, the background is that based on the tax treaty between the US and Dutch governments, the Dutch tax authorities levy a 15% withholding tax on dividends paid by Dutch domiciled corporations. Consequently, no direct cash refund is available from the Dutch tax authorities, but check the possibilities with your tax advisor for a foreign tax credit in your personal US income tax return based on your specific facts and circumstances.
